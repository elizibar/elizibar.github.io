# F.E.A.R.: Perseus Mandate

**System**: PC  
**Release Date**: November 6, 2007  
**Played During**: FEARtober 2018

### Verdict

Perseus Mandate is the second F.E.A.R. expansion pack, and the one that ventures into different territory than the original. You don't play as the Pointman, you play as another super-soldier type figure, which makes this game feel more like a trial-run for F.E.A.R 2: Project Origin.

The events of Perseus Mandate take place during the events of F.E.A.R., very similar to how Project Origin does. The more I think about this game, the more I think it was the original draft plot for Project Origin, before F.E.A.R. 2 got a bit weirder.

Like Extraction Point, Perseus Mandate features new toys to play with. I remember the lightning gun and have a good time with it. The story picks up with the same government official that Aristide was talking to, sending people to go get "Perseus" and you just happen to run into those people as you infiltrate into an Armacham facility.. Along the way you'll deal with the sort of horror shenanigans anyone should come to expect of F.E.A.R., from creepy atmosphere to jump scares, and you'll fight through waves of enemy troops driven by some of the best enemy AI seen in gaming.

Eventually you start battling crazy mercenary dudes over "Perseus" which is apparently DNA. There's a couple of really hard fights near the end of the game, probably with the last boss being the hardest thing to kill out of the games based on the original F.E.A.R.

It's a fun time, but feels a little thin and isn't as well crafted as the base game or Extraction Point. The new toys help mix it up, but even so I remember in the FEARtober 2018 playthrough thinking that it was starting to feel a bit same-y. I wish there was a way to port the expansion weapons back to the base game, they could be fun additions.

There's not really any other shooter quite like the original F.E.A.R. and this is a solid, if not spectacular, continuation of it.
