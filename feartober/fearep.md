# F.E.A.R.: Extraction Point

**System**: PC  
**Release Date**: October 24, 2006  
**Played During**: FEARtober 2018

### Verdict

F.E.A.R. Extraction Point is the first "expansion pack" (as we used to call these things before it was all called DLC). It picks right up after the original game, when the helicopter at the end of F.E.A.R. crashes. At the time it cost $30, which for an experience that I recall being 3 or so long seems steep compared to the full experience of the first game. It brings over the same solid slo-mo shooting experience with a great variety of weapons, and then even adds a few more weapons on top including the new laser that is a joy to shoot at everything.

I can't quite remember if I played this expansion when it was new, or if I played it a year or so later, but I got a lot of joy out of it. The second time I played through it would have been during FEARtober 2018 (see the playlist below).

After the crash, the Pointman, Jin, and the delta force guy find themselves somehow alive and in a deserted part of town. They'll have to escape. And worse for all of them, Paxton Fettle is back, and alive. If this sounds a bit like F.E.A.R. 3 in that regard with the return of Fettle, I believe the team that developed this game also developed 3. From here it's a lot of the same action as the base game, only slightly less well done, with Alma showing up, disintegrating Replicas, Paxton mind-controlling his army into attacking you, and lots of mysterious things happening.

The ending is kind of obscure, and the status of the story in the canon is denied by Monolith, but I'm not sure there's a lot here that contradicts what happens in F.E.A.R. 2: Project Origin. Given the lapse between this game and F.E.A.R. 3's events, and how Project Origin focuses on a different character entirely than the Pointman and Fettle, I don't see that this story doesn't fit more or less cozily into the overall F.E.A.R. timeline. Heck, what happens with Alma near the end of this one feels like a way that sort of sets up what Alma does in Project Origin and F.E.A.R. 3.

In the end, I enjoy Extraction Point a lot. It adds new weapons, some whole new abilities (knocking doors open or cracking crates), some new scares, and continues all the same things that made the original game so good. It's a solid play, and these days not having to pay its original retail price takes what little complaint I could make out of it.
