# Yeah Mick

**Type:** Sound / Normal  
**Classification:** Affirmation Mickémon  
**Mickédex Number:** #156  
**Height:** 2’00” (0.6 m)  
**Weight:** 24.3 lbs (11.0 kg)

### Short Mickédex Entry

"The Affirmation Mickémon. It can only say one word, and it says it with absolute conviction."

### Biology

Yeah Mick is a Mickus that has been permanently affected by the Yeah Zone deep in the jungles of Discootia. Its body still carries the classic potato-gremlin shape, signature hat, and mullet, but it now radiates an almost hypnotic positive energy. It can only speak the word "Yeah," yet somehow manages to convey entire conversations, battle strategies, and emotional support through tone, volume, and enthusiastic body language alone.

It is extremely encouraging to its Trainer and allies, constantly affirming every action with a loud, heartfelt “YEAH!” Even when things go wrong, it remains unshakably positive. In battle it uses Sound-type moves that boost its allies’ confidence while confusing opponents who keep hearing the same word over and over.

### Evolution

Evolves from Mickus when it reaches the Yeah Zone in the jungles of Discootia.

**Previous Evolution:** Mickus (Yeah Zone)  
**Next Evolution(s):** –
