# Frostymick

**Type:** Ice / Fairy  
**Classification:** Living Snowman Mickémon  
**Mickédex Number:** #164  
**Height:** 3'03" (1.0 m)  
**Weight:** 54.2 lbs (24.6 kg)  

### Short Mickédex Entry
"The Living Snowman Mickémon. It is three perfect snowballs stacked on top of each other, held together by pure determination and a slightly crooked smile."

### Biology
Frostymick evolves from Snowmick when it levels up while knowing a Powder move. Its body becomes three distinct snowballs stacked into a classic snowman shape. The bottom ball is the largest, the middle one slightly smaller, and the head the smallest. A proper carrot nose, two pieces of coal for eyes, and a wide, slightly lopsided grin complete the look. Its hat has become a simple black top hat that sits at a jaunty angle, and a short, scarf-like mullet of snow trails behind it.

Despite looking like it could fall apart at any moment, Frostymick is surprisingly sturdy. It leaves a trail of perfect little snowballs wherever it walks and has a habit of waving at Trainers with a stick arm that wasn’t there a second ago. It radiates a gentle, cheerful cold and seems happiest when children are nearby.

### Evolution
Evolves from Snowmick when it levels up while knowing a Powder move.

**Previous Evolution:** Snowmick (Level up while knowing a Powder move)  
**Next Evolution(s):** None
