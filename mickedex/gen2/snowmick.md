# Snowmick

**Type:** Ice / Normal  
**Classification:** Fresh Snow Mickémon  
**Mickédex Number:** #163  
**Height:** 2'02" (0.7 m)  
**Weight:** 29.8 lbs (13.5 kg)  

### Short Mickédex Entry
"The Fresh Snow Mickémon. Its body looks like it was packed together by a child who got distracted halfway through."

### Biology
Snowmick evolves from Wintermick when it levels up during snowy weather. The heavy red furs are replaced by a soft, slightly uneven coat of pure white snow. Its hat becomes a simple, slightly lopsided snow-covered beanie, and two stubby stick-like arms poke out from its sides. A short carrot-like nose and a wide, blank expression complete the look of a snowman that was built with more enthusiasm than skill.

It leaves little trails of powdery snow wherever it walks and seems completely unbothered by cold. When happy it will sometimes spontaneously form tiny snowballs in its hands and then forget what it was going to do with them.

### Evolution
Evolves from Wintermick when it levels up during snowy weather.

It can then evolve into Frostymick if it levels up while knowing a Powder move.  
It can evolve into Northmick if it levels up at the Peak of Mount Lucas at night.

**Previous Evolution:** Wintermick (Level up in snowy weather)  
**Next Evolution(s):** Frostymick (Level up while knowing a Powder move), Northmick (Level up at Peak of Mount Lucas at night)
