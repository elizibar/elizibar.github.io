# Mickolaus

**Type:** Ice / Fairy  
**Classification:** Gift-Bringer Mickémon  
**Mickédex Number:** #161  
**Height:** 3'07" (1.1 m)  
**Weight:** 78.3 lbs (35.5 kg)  

### Short Mickédex Entry
"The Gift-Bringer Mickémon. It carries a sack that is somehow always full, though no one has ever seen it restock."

### Biology
Mickolaus is the final festive evolution of the Jinglemick line. Its body becomes taller and rounder, covered in a thick red coat with white fur trim. A full, fluffy white beard frames its face, and its signature hat has grown into a long, floppy Santa-style cap that still somehow manages to look slightly too big for its head. A heavy sack is permanently slung over one shoulder; the contents of the sack change depending on who is looking at it, but it is always something the viewer would like to receive.

Despite its grand appearance, Mickolaus retains the same derpy eyes and mild confusion of its pre-evolutions. It takes the job of gift-giving very seriously, even if it occasionally hands out the wrong present with complete confidence.

### Evolution
Evolves from Jinglemick when it levels up at the Peak of Mount Lucas during a snowstorm.

**Previous Evolution:** Jinglemick (Level up at Peak of Mount Lucas during snowstorm)  
**Next Evolution(s):** None
