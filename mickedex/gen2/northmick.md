# Northmick

**Type:** Ice / Steel  
**Classification:** Deep Winter Mickémon  
**Mickédex Number:** #165  
**Height:** 3'11" (1.2 m)  
**Weight:** 89.5 lbs (40.6 kg)  

### Short Mickédex Entry
"The Deep Winter Mickémon. It stands motionless for hours on the highest peaks, watching the horizon as if waiting for something only it can see."

### Biology
Northmick evolves from Snowmick when it levels up at the Peak of Mount Lucas at night. The soft snow of its previous form hardens into a dense, blue-white coat that looks almost metallic in the moonlight. Its body becomes taller and more solid, with broad shoulders and thick limbs. A heavy, frost-covered cloak hangs from its shoulders, and its hat has transformed into a tall, pointed hood rimmed with ice crystals. Its eyes glow a pale icy blue, giving it a solemn, watchful expression that still somehow retains a hint of the classic Mickus derpiness underneath.

Northmick rarely speaks or moves unless necessary. It is often found standing perfectly still on mountain ridges, facing the wind. Trainers report that simply being near one makes the air feel clearer and colder, and that lost climbers have been guided back to safety by following the faint blue glow of its eyes through whiteout conditions.

### Evolution
Evolves from Snowmick when it levels up at the Peak of Mount Lucas at night.
