# Jinglemick

**Type:** Ice / Sound  
**Classification:** Bell-Ringer Mickémon  
**Mickédex Number:** #160  
**Height:** 2'03" (0.7 m)  
**Weight:** 31.9 lbs (14.5 kg)  

### Short Mickédex Entry
"The Bell-Ringer Mickémon. Every movement produces a soft, cheerful jingle. It seems incapable of sneaking up on anyone."

### Biology
Jinglemick evolves from Wintermick when the bond with its Trainer grows strong enough. The heavy red furs thin out into a lighter, more festive coat, and its trapper hat is replaced by a soft pointed hood lined with tiny golden bells. A cluster of larger bells hangs from the tip of its mullet and from a small sash around its middle.

It constantly emits a gentle jingling sound with every step, hop, or head-tilt. The noise is never loud enough to be annoying, but it is persistent enough that Trainers always know exactly where their Jinglemick is. In battle the bells amplify Sound-type moves, creating bright, ringing attacks that can disorient opponents while boosting the spirits of its allies.

Despite the constant noise, Jinglemick remains as cheerfully derpy as the rest of its line, often tilting its head and setting off a cascade of tiny bells when confused.

### Evolution
Jinglemick evolves from Wintermick when friendship is high.

It can then evolve into Mickolaus if it levels up at the Peak of Mount Lucas during a snowstorm.  
It can evolve into Mickletoe if it levels up while holding a Mistletoe.

**Previous Evolution:** Wintermick (High Friendship)  
**Next Evolution(s):** Mickolaus (Level up at Peak of Mount Lucas during snowstorm), Mickletoe (Level up while holding Mistletoe)
