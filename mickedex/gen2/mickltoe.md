# Mickletoe

**Type:** Grass / Fairy  
**Classification:** Hanging Berry Mickémon  
**Mickédex Number:** #162  
**Height:** 2'01" (0.6 m)  
**Weight:** 19.8 lbs (9.0 kg)  

### Short Mickédex Entry
"The Hanging Berry Mickémon. It prefers to dangle from doorways and low branches, waiting for someone to walk underneath."

### Biology
Mickletoe is a lighter, more delicate evolution of Jinglemick. Its body remains small and potato-shaped, but it is now covered in soft green leaves with clusters of pale white berries growing from its head and shoulders. A sprig of actual mistletoe is permanently stuck in its hat, and its mullet has turned into trailing vines that help it hang upside-down from door frames, tree branches, or the edges of roofs.

It is surprisingly persistent about its hanging habit. Trainers often find their Mickletoe suspended above doorways in the morning, staring down with a hopeful expression. It is said that walking under a Mickletoe brings a small burst of good luck, though this may simply be the result of the Mickémon dropping one of its berries on your head.

### Evolution
Evolves from Jinglemick when it levels up while holding a Mistletoe.

**Previous Evolution:** Jinglemick (Level up while holding Mistletoe)  
**Next Evolution(s):** None
