# Amickforallseasons

**Type:** Normal / Fairy  
**Classification:** All-Season Mickémon  
**Mickédex Number:** #158  
**Height:** 2'00" (0.6 m)  
**Weight:** 28.6 lbs (13.0 kg)  

### Short Mickédex Entry
"The All-Season Mickémon. A Mickus that has somehow absorbed the spirit of every holiday at once. It never quite knows which season it’s supposed to be celebrating, so it just does all of them simultaneously."

### Biology
Amickforallseasons is a Mickus that has become a living calendar of celebration. Its classic potato-gremlin body remains, but it is now covered in a chaotic mix of holiday decorations. Its trucker hat features a tiny spinning calendar that flips between pumpkins, Christmas trees, hearts, and other seasonal icons. One eye glows like a jack-o’-lantern while the other twinkles with multicolored Christmas lights. A long patchwork scarf knitted from leftover Halloween and Christmas stockings wraps around its neck, and its mullet is a tangled mess of candy-cane stripes, tiny bat wings, tinsel, and autumn leaves.

It only appears when the calendar itself seems confused. Trainers who stand near one often find themselves simultaneously craving candy corn and hot cocoa. Despite the sensory overload it carries, Amickforallseasons remains cheerfully overwhelmed rather than distressed, greeting every season with the same derpy enthusiasm.

### Evolution
A group of Professors have seeded special **Season Stones** throughout the Cordia Region. When a Mickus (or Mickey, Bigmick, or Minimick) uses a Season Stone, it evolves into Amickforallseasons.

From there the line branches according to where the Trainer takes it:

- Visiting the **Peak of Mount Lucas** causes it to evolve into **Wintermick** (Christmas / Winter line).  
- Visiting the **Mickatoa Caldera** causes it to evolve into **Autumnick** (Halloween / Autumn line).

**Previous Evolution:** Mickus (Season Stone)  
**Next Evolution(s):** Wintermick (Peak of Mount Lucas), Autumnick (Mickatoa Caldera)
