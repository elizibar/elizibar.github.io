# Mickshrimp

**Type:** Water / Heart  
**Classification:** Shrimp Mickémon  
**Mickédex Number:** #095  
**Height:** 1'04" (0.4 m)  
**Weight:** 8.8 lbs (4.0 kg)  

### Short Mickédex Entry

"The Shrimp Mickémon. Mickshrimp is a little goober shrimpy guy that loves frying rice and is obsessed with octopuses and squids."

### Biology
Mickshrimp is a tiny, derpy shrimp Mickus with a soft pink shell, big googly eyes, and the classic green-and-blue baseball cap perched on its head. It carries a tiny frying pan and is constantly cooking rice, often muttering "They never believe a shrimp fried this rice" under its breath.

It has a strange fixation on octopuses and squids (especially Micktopus and Mickraken), treating them like celebrities. Despite its small size, it is surprisingly fiery when cooking and can launch hot fried rice at opponents. It is cheerful, slightly chaotic, and very proud of its culinary skills.

### Evolution
Evolves from Mick U after participating with a Micktopus or Mickraken and winning.

**Previous Evolution:** Mick U (Micktopus or Mickraken)
