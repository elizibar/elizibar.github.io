# Final Fantasy VI Pixel Remaster

**System**: Steam (PC)  
**Release Date**: February 23, 2022  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy VI Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff6series/)  
**Date Played**: May 17, 2026 through June 3, 2026  

### Verdict

Loved it. So much fun to play.

The story stands out hard. You start the game blind to the world, and by the halfway point you are swearing revenge against a tyrannical god. That progression is huge, and the cast is full of great characters - each with their own quirks and roles so none of them feel redundant. If I had to pick three peak moments: the opera scene, the transition between worlds, and the final fight. The rest of the game is packed with memorable fights, mechanical variety across the party, and character beats that stick.

Expectations were high going in. The Pixel Remaster features helped it clear even that bar. Presentation is superb - music, graphics, menus, all of it. I would absolutely recommend the game, and this version in particular. It is missing some of the GBA version’s extra boss fights, which is a shame, but what is here looks and plays great.

If you take your time and explore, it is a pretty easy game outside of a few select encounters. Overleveling is easy, and once you understand how Espers affect character growth the whole thing becomes very manageable. Soundtrack and presentation are immaculate.

Relative to the rest of the SNES era, this is a top-2 JRPG on the system. Final Fantasy IV is close behind it; the real competition for the crown is Chrono Trigger.

I might replay Final Fantasy VI someday, but if I do it will be to try a different version. Grinding the Steam achievements is a bit of a chore, though less painful than Final Fantasy III’s. Missing a treasure chest in a one-time visit area and losing an achievement for it still sucks.
