# Final Fantasy Mystic Quest

**System**: Super NES  
**Release Date**: October 1992  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy Mystic Quest](/yearoffinalfantasy/ffmqseries/)  
**Date Played**: May 3, 2026 to May 6, 2026

### Verdict

Mystic Quest is a lot of fun and I like it for what it is. The simplified mechanics and limited party size streamline the experience, but they can also lead to some frustrating combat encounters. Having played it years ago I knew what to expect going in, and it met those expectations and gave me a pretty fun time with some good music.

I really like this game as a fun little dungeon crawler, so I think people should play it, though I'm not quite sure who the audience is anymore. Going through that tower that connects the different realms sticks out - in my mind it links Mystic Quest to the SaGa / Final Fantasy Legend games, as well as FF1 and FF4. All these games with a giant ancient tower feel like they could be part of some cohesive metaverse.

There are times when you'll be fighting an enemy that can paralyze you and you just get stun-locked until you die. It feels unfair. If it didn't have "Final Fantasy" in the name, you'd be forgiven for not knowing it was a Final Fantasy game. The enemy designs, the music, almost everything is different from the main series.

The soundtrack has a couple of bangers and the menus are easy to navigate. Difficulty is easy to modest and the pacing doesn't waste your time. The story is simplistic but it's enough to drive the game forward, and some of the NPC characters that join the party get their own little mini-arcs that feel satisfying.

I've played it three or four times over the years. I think I'm good. The optional content is mostly those convenient battlefield areas with a set number of encounters that give XP, money, and loot. They're pretty quick and easy and not too big of an investment.
