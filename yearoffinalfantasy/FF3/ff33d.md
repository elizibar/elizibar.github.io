# Final Fantasy III 3D Remaster

**System**: Steam (PC)  
**Release Date**: May 27, 2014  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy III Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff3series/)  
**Date Played**: February 2, 2026 through February 21, 2026  

### Verdict

Final Fantasy III is probably the biggest slog of the first three games, and the 3D remaster somehow feels even slower than I remember the NES version being. There are things I like about it, but overall it left me with a very mixed feeling.

The job system is the real highlight. Final Fantasy I locked you into fixed classes, Final Fantasy II let characters do everything, and III is the one that lets you freely change the party to meet different challenges. That flexibility is still satisfying. On the downside, several jobs feel like one-trick ponies meant for a specific moment (Scholar is the clearest example), so the actual variety in useful party setups is more limited than it first appears.

Some of the 3D models and environments look surprisingly good. Combat, though, felt even slower to resolve than I wanted, and the menus in this version are clunky. The soundtrack is mostly forgettable outside of "Roaming Sheep," which is the one track that still sticks with me.

Difficulty is all over the place. Bring the right jobs and many areas become trivial; then the game suddenly demands you turn the party into frogs or inflict Mini to progress, forcing a full rebuild. The constant new movement methods on the overworld (and having to swap between different airships) also got annoying. The early airship ram is a fun, memorable moment, but the later game wears out its welcome. By the end I was not having a lot of fun and wrote an AutoHotkey script to handle grinding for me after coming straight off the already grindy Final Fantasy II.

The story feels thin. The early stretch is fine, but the villain’s scheme never became compelling and the whole thing grows increasingly meh as it goes.

I cannot recommend this version. I have not played the Pixel Remaster yet, but if it has the same quality-of-life features as the other Pixel Remasters I expect it plays better. I will not replay this 3D version; it holds nothing for me. The achievements are especially unfun, requiring you to level every job on every character. I did the optional superboss (after more scripted grinding) but freely admit I downloaded a save to pop the final job-mastery achievement rather than let the script run for that long.
