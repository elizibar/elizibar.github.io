# Final Fantasy XII: The Zodiac Age

**System**: Steam (PC)  
**Release Date**: February 1, 2018  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy XII Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff12series/), [Ivalice Alliance](/yearoffinalfantasy/ivalice/)  
**Date Played**: July 27, 2025 through August 24, 2025  

### Verdict

Loved it.

Being able to sit at a higher level and design how the party reacts to fights felt liberating compared to the micromanagement other Final Fantasy games demand. It plays a bit like an MMO where you are the entire party. Mastering the Gambit system is really the core of the experience.

I remembered enjoying Final Fantasy XII years ago when it was new, but I never finished it - I made it to Archades and then never picked it up again. Playing through the rest this time, it was better than I remembered, or maybe the quality-of-life features in The Zodiac Age were just that good. This really does seem like the best way to play FF12.

Exploration still gives a real sense of wonder and mystery sometimes. Entering a new area means never quite knowing what to expect or how it will connect to everything else. The soundtrack has some great pieces, and the updated License Board in this version feels better than the original grid.

A few things annoy me. Rare enemies and some of the hunts have stringent, if not outright silly, spawn requirements, and those can feel more like chores than fun. Some of those same fights are a slog. Balthier, whose signature weapon is a gun, somehow has the slowest attack animations while using one.

Difficulty in the main story isn’t a big deal. The real challenge sits in the rare hunts and optional content. Story is a hoot. Your perspective characters (Vaan and Penelo) aren’t the main characters, so when they get dropped into plot points they can ask the dumb questions and fill the player in. Balthier is the leading man, of course, and Fran probably has the most fanart of anyone in the game.

It’s a refreshing take on the genre. We will be revisiting later to finish off the Bestiary. The achievements seem very doable - hunting rare monsters is already a core loop, so filling out the bestiary is incentivized naturally.
