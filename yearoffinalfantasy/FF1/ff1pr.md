# Final Fantasy Pixel Remaster

**System**: Steam (PC)  
**Release Date**: July 28, 2021  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff1series/)  
**Date Played**: January 19, 2026 through January 25, 2026  

### Verdict

I loved the Final Fantasy Pixel Remaster. The quality-of-life features stood out the most - having a proper map and adjustable XP rates took a lot of the original NES version’s frustration out while still keeping the core experience intact. I went in with high but modest expectations and ended up having even more fun than I thought I would.

The difficulty curve can still surprise you if you’re not ready for it. Some enemies come with instant-death or petrification attacks that can spiral out of control quickly. On the flip side, using items like the Zeus Gauntlet for infinite magic was a blast and let the Black Mage really shine at crowd control. In the first Final Fantasy, magic users are more about cleaning up groups while physical attackers handle the big single-target damage. Once you understand that (and how buffs like Temper work), bosses like Warmech and Tiamat become much more manageable.

The rearranged soundtrack is excellent and the pixel art looks clean and colorful. The game is less grindy than Final Fantasy II or III, and playing it brought back a strong wave of childhood nostalgia. The time-loop story is still cool - not something you see often in fiction, let alone an RPG (7th Saga is the only other one that comes to mind for me).

The main downside is the missing bonus content from earlier versions like the GBA and PSP releases. I’m also not a fan of the achievements that demand 100% treasure chests or a complete bestiary, since they encourage a lot of backtracking. Finding Warmech was especially annoying due to the low encounter rate, though at least nothing permanently locked you out of progress.

I would recommend this version. It smooths over the rough edges of the original without losing what makes the game special, and it respects the player’s time. I doubt I’ll replay it soon, but if I do it should be a quick run - and it might be fun to try a different party than my usual Fighter / Red Mage / Black Mage / White Mage setup.
