# Final Fantasy: Legend of the Crystals

**System**: OVA (VHS)  
**Release Date**: March 21, 1994 (Japan; four episodes through July 1994)  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy V Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff5series/)  
**Date Watched**: April 30, 2026  

### Verdict

Fun, fluffy, not particularly deep, and it leans into comedy a bit hard. Watching it a second time during the Year of Final Fantasy was basically a new experience; I did not remember much of it.

Not a lot stood out either way. I had a dim recollection of not liking it much, but I came out of the rewatch entertained, so it cleared that low bar. I still would not recommend it all that strongly. It does not feel especially like a Final Fantasy show, so series fans do not get much of a hook, and it is not distinctive enough for non-fans to have a reason to seek it out.

Around the halfway point I kept thinking it was basically retelling the Final Fantasy V plot, only worse. I am not sure the FF5 tie-in was even necessary. If you never mentioned that Linally is descended from Bartz and you changed a few place names, the experience would be almost identical.

Overall it is pretty mediocre. It looks decent enough for the time, and the English dub voices were okay (I kept playing "which Tenchi character is that?" while listening). It feels like it could have used a little more runtime. The story is mostly a slapstick retread of Final Fantasy V’s plot. I do not feel any need to watch it again.

Wikipedia mentions a manga adaptation of the same story. I do not know anything about it, but it might be worth tracking down sometime.
