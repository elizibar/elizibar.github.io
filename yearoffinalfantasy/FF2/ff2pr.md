# Final Fantasy II Pixel Remaster

**System**: Steam (PC)  
**Release Date**: July 28, 2021  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy II Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff2series/)  
**Date Played**: January 28, 2026 through February 1, 2026  

### Verdict

The Pixel Remaster smooths over some of the chunkier parts of the original experience (the mini-maps in particular are a nice addition) without changing the core Final Fantasy II feel. I liked the look of it - modernized just enough to feel fresh compared to the NES version while still reading as the same game.

I went in expecting the usual early slog of power-leveling followed by steamrolling most of the rest of the game, and that is mostly what happened. One genuine quality-of-life improvement in this version is that some stats increase without having to deliberately grind for them, which helps a bit. Even so, the underlying systems are still a mess. You end up making strange decisions like punching your own party to raise HP or camping on Captain enemies for hours at the start. Everything - including spells - levels through successful use, so the game remains one long grind.

If you know how the systems work, the game becomes trivial. If you do not, it can feel like a wall. This might be the roughest Final Fantasy experience for someone who goes in cold. The soundtrack did not stick with me at all; I cannot recall a single tune. Presentation in the Pixel Remaster is still solid overall.

Story-wise this is the first Final Fantasy that really tries to tell one, with guest characters rotating in until you settle on a more permanent party. You can feel Square figuring out storytelling as they go.

I would recommend this version - it is the best way to play FF2 right now - but I have no plans to replay it (or any other version) unless there is a good external reason. The achievements are more forgiving than I feared; you only need to raise one weapon or spell to level 16 rather than everything on every character, which cuts the late grind down considerably.

Amusing side note from the first time I played the NES version years ago: my save got corrupted and left me with characters holding nonsense items and weird stats. It ended up feeling a lot like I had already finished the early Captain grind, just with party members that looked completely broken in the menus.
