# Final Fantasy VII: Advent Children

**System**: DVD  
**Release Date**: April 24, 2005  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy VII Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff7series/)  
**Date Watched**: July 23, 2026  

### Verdict

Lots of great action, but the plot comes at you kind of out of nowhere with minimal explanation. It throws a lot at you - Geostigma, the Remnants - and doesn't do much to explain them even as it's going on. The novels go into a lot more of that. I feel like a little more explanation would have helped make it flow more smoothly, like a quick recap of FF7 or something.

I remember just thinking it was okay from watching it years ago, but the fights really left me feeling good about it after it was over. Great fight scenes. The Tifa fight in the church is peak action, and Monty Oum's career was born out of that moment. Shame that he didn't get to keep making more RWBY.

Unlike the Legend of the Crystals anime, this is an easy recommendation to an FF7 fan. Revisiting the characters one more time was a lot of fun.

It starts slow, but really picks up speed until the last hour or so is one long action sequence. Almost exhausting, the same way John Wick 4 is. The movie really drives on its action, and not so much the story. It feels a bit like it jumps around without explaining the overarching setup.

Yeah, I'd rewatch Advent Children.
