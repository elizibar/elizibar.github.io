# Final Fantasy VII Remake

**System**: PS4  
**Release Date**: April 10, 2020  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy VII Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff7series/)  
**Date Played**: April 11, 2020 through October 11, 2020  

### Verdict

I loved playing through FF7 Remake. There's maybe a few gripes I could muster, but overall it was a lot of fun. It's a really pretty game, and combat felt fresh and fun. I had really high expectations and they were exceeded.

I think I can recommend this game to everyone that's not a stick in the mud stuck in the year 1997. For whatever reason I see so much hate for this game on Twitter/X and I really don't get it. It is entirely possible for people to dislike something without pooping all over it, but that doesn't get views I guess.

The FF7 intro sequence is just as memorable here in higher fidelity as it is in the base game. I almost forgot to mention the wonderful addition of Aerith with the chair-shot on a henchman in Wall Market. Lots of great moments pop out as you play it, and the game mechanics feel fluid. With the Midgar section expanded out like this, some of the writing falls a bit flat and uninspired.

It was a lot of fun. It's doing its own thing, and maybe that's why people hate on it. It may have "Remake" in the name, but it's clear it's not a strict remake at all within an hour or so of playing. It's more like a sequel or the start of an FF7 multiverse.

The soundtrack slapped, the GUIs are immaculate, and some of the details in the animations are just wonderful - like watching Aerith's facial reactions at various points. Difficulty seemed fine. I don't think I ground out too much early on and didn't run into too many bumps, though there were a few fights that did take some rethinking or simple bull-headed brute-forcing to overcome.

Story was good. I haven't played the sequel or Intergrade yet so I don't know how the whole "defying fate" thing is going to factor into the story, but this feels more like Sephiroth is tormenting Cloud as opposed to Sephiroth being the big faceless boogieman as he was in FF7 originally.

I plan to play the Intergrade version as part of this project. I originally played on PS4 and I think I'm missing one or two trophies. They seem doable, but I just didn't enjoy things like the exercise minigame enough to want to grind for the trophies obsessively.

A couple of anecdotes: when this first came out, they did a partnership with Butterfinger, so I bought a few candy bars for my mother and took the codes for myself. Also when this was first announced, my father prepurchased it for me as a gift, and it came out years later after he passed away, making it a final, unexpected gift from him.
