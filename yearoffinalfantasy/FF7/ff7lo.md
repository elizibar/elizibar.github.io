# Last Order: Final Fantasy VII

**System**: DVD  
**Release Date**: September 14, 2005  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy VII Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff7series/)  
**Date Played**: July 23, 2026  

### Verdict

Last Order is a retelling of the core cutscenes from Final Fantasy VII, and it succeeds at doing that one thing, but as a standalone bit of media it doesn't stand up. There's no real "story" here, just the middle of a story. I really liked how badass it made Zack look.

I couldn't actually remember how I felt about it before, so I went in with minimal expectations and came out feeling pretty good. I think this is only really good for people who are fans of FF7. As a stand-alone story it doesn't have much going for it.

There are two pieces to this story: the Nibelheim reactor where Sephiroth goes nuts, and Zack's escape from Nibelheim. Both are effectively climaxes to a story that we're not really told, and the ending of both sequences is good. It really feels like we needed more of a framing story to bring context. The story cuts back and forth between two different plot threads, which makes it confusing for those who don't already know the story.

I don't know that I really feel the need to watch this again.
