### Verdict

I ended up having a blast with this book. Seeing how Kadaj and the other villains from Advent Children started out, and just how crazy they are, was great - and also a surprise. I never could have guessed the story would go there.

I didn't know what to think going in, and the opening chapter or two were rough, but once the story got rolling I really loved it. Yeah, I think this is a solid recommendation for anyone who wants to see how Advent Children comes about from the perspective of the Turks and Shinra instead of the main cast.

I really like how this helped give a great sense of what life is like in the FF7 world post-Meteor. Checking back in on Don Corneo was also a nice bit. The main character is a little bit more of an idiot than I would have liked. But if he hadn't been so dumb, I guess the story would have been very different.

Unlike On the Way to a Smile, this at least told a complete story with a beginning, middle, and end. It starts slow, but gets rolling good and fast. The main characters are all weird and eccentric. Maybe a bit too much.
