# Final Fantasy VII: Snowboarding

**System**: VCAST Cellphone  
**Release Date**: March 15, 2005  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy VII Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff7series/)  
**Date Played**: September 13, 2026

### Verdict

It's okay. It's the FF7 Snowboarding minigame on a phone. I'm surprised how well it controls - it actually felt more responsive than the original minigame. If you're into the minigame go for it. If you're me, not so much. (I'm bad at it.)

I'm surprised cellphones from the mid 2000s were able to push polygons so well. Hard as snowboarding ever has been. (I'm bad at it.) If there's a way to unlock some of the other FF7 characters, I don't know how, but that might've been a nice touch.

No desire to ever play this again. I'm also surprised that a simple experience like this wasn't ported forward onto Android and iOS. Seems fine for what it is.
