### Verdict

My feelings for this game swung around a lot while playing it. Overall I came out disappointed, mostly because so much of it just did not feel good to play.

The dialogue is by and large awful. Large stretches of the plot do not make much sense either. Where exactly did President Shinra get the money and super-technology to field a whole army of mako/Jenova-altered supersoldiers with vehicles and weapons that never showed up in the original Final Fantasy VII? The story is ludicrous on its face: a dead villain from VII somehow survives, and Shinra quietly built a massive black-ops army that the original game never hinted at. There are a million cutscenes, most of them filled with dialogue that is neither fun nor interesting. If Vincent had not been voiced by Spike Spiegel’s VA I might have skipped them entirely.

As a shooter it does not feel good. Aiming is a pain. The longer I played, the more it felt like the game should have just used a lock-on system so mobility could matter more. Vincent slides, leaps, and glides through cutscenes, then plays like a tank in actual gameplay. The controls stay clunky the whole way. Compared to a normal JRPG experience, this is a poor shooter, and there is not enough story juice for a Final Fantasy fan to really bite into.

Things did improve late. Once I reached the final area, started using the SUPER GUN, and the pace picked up, the game suddenly felt faster and flowed a lot better. Difficulty itself was mostly fine; aside from the Rosso fight it never felt frustratingly hard even when I died a few times. The soundtrack slapped, though the constant loading screens undercut the momentum.

I will not replay this. Thank goodness there are no Steam achievements for a PS2 game, because that is the only thing that might have pushed me into the optional special missions and collectible hunting. We did grab enough of the collectibles on this playthrough to unlock the secret ending, so at least we got to see Gackt show up as "G" for thirty seconds. That was cool.

Expectations going in were middling. The game mostly failed to meet them.
