# Final Fantasy VII: On the Way to a Smile

**System**: Book  
**Release Date**: October 30, 2018  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy VII Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff7series/)  
**Date Played**: July 26, 2026 through Early-August 2026  

### Verdict

Solid way to bridge the gap between Final Fantasy VII and Advent Children, but as a standalone story it doesn't succeed so well. It gives a good recap and touches on a lot of the cast, focusing on one character at a time in each chapter. Case Denzel is probably the best story, but the characters are all written as individuals - none of them feel or sound the same in their stories.

No expectations going in, just some good fun. I think it's solid reading for a Final Fantasy fan, but not so great for non-fans.

Red XIII's whole chapter was a different perspective and really showed his struggle to bridge the different parts of his being. Getting more time with Barret to understand why he left Marlene with Tifa and how he went off to dig for oil was great as well.

While there's a lot of individual mini-stories, this book doesn't tell its own whole story. It just covers the way between two stories.
