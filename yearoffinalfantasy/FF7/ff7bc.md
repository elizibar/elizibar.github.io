# Before Crisis: Final Fantasy VII

**System**: FOMA/DoCoMo Cellphones  
**Release Date**: September 24, 2004  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy VII Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff7series/)  
**Date Played**: September 13, 2026  

### Verdict

It seems neat, but since it was Japanese I couldn't really understand what was going on, and the combat seemed odd and I wasn't good at it. The combat was rough. Weirdly real-time, and very positional - it seemed hard to pick up. I didn't like it.

Not really a recommendation, unless you can read Japanese. I'm sure the fan remakes are probably a better experience. Dude, we got bodied by the first enemy in the game. I will forever remember how much I sucked at this.

Seems like normal Final Fantasy turn-based or ATB combat would've been a better fit for mobile. Between this and The After Years, that mid-2000s mobile FF experience seems lacklustre. I know the story because of Ever Crisis, so I know it gets pretty good and crazy.

It took me longer to get this up and running than the amount of time we spent playing it.
