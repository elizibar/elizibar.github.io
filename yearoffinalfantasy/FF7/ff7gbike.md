# Final Fantasy VII G-Bike

**System**: Android / iOS  
**Release Date**: October 30, 2014  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy VII Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff7series/)  
**Date Played**:  

### Verdict

Japan-only free-to-play mobile racing game based on the original FF7 bike minigame. Servers shut down on December 15, 2015. APKs still exist but the game requires its online servers and stops at the title screen. No working offline version or private server is available. Unplayable.
