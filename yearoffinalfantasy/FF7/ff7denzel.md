# On the Way to a Smile - Episode: Denzel

**System**: DVD  
**Release Date**: April 16, 2009  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy VII Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff7series/)  
**Date Played**: July 23, 2026  

### Verdict

I wasn't quite sure where this was going at first, but once it started developing I thought it was a charming and thoughtful way to bridge the story between FF7 and Advent Children. The story is tragic, but hopeful. I didn't know what to expect and left charmed by it.

Really feels like it's a key part of the Advent Children story, and it's weird that it's not more prominent. The whole beginning sequence, between the plate drop and Meteor, was rough to watch, but the ending when Tseng reveals who was helping to take care of Denzel after the plate drop was the moment that tied the entire experience together.

Provides important context for Advent Children.
