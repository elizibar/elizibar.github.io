# Final Fantasy VII (2013 Re-release)

**System**: Steam (PC)  
**Release Date**: July 24, 2013  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy VII Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff7series/)  
**Date Played**: June 10, 2026 through July 11, 2026  

### Verdict

While the quality-of-life features in this version aren't as extensive as what the Pixel Remasters gave the earlier games, the addition of a few things like speeding up combat made it smoother to play than the original PSX release. It took what was already great and elevated it a bit higher.

By 2026 standards the graphics are pretty clunky, so that's a negative, but the pre-rendered backgrounds still provide great atmosphere. It holds up, mostly. I had high hopes going in but wasn't sure how I'd feel replaying it 20-ish years later. It was a blast. I had a good old time remembering the game as I went through it.

If you're going to play it on PC, this seems like the version to go with.

Wall Market, Shinra Tower, the motorcycle chase, Cosmo Canyon, the Ancient Capital - there are so many moments that stand out it's hard to name them all. The game also has a million different mini-games that help break up the JRPG gameplay and keep things fresh, plus tons of secrets and little sidequests crammed into every nook and cranny.

Grinding AP to level materia feels like a chore. It's easy to overlevel characters just from trying to power up your magic. The menus are kind of clunky in places too, which is a regression from how smooth FF6's menus were. Soundtrack is legendary, though.

FF7 is a pretty easy game except for a couple of early bosses and the optional super bosses. Some of the characters could have used more screentime, but they're all great. The story is convoluted because of all the unreliable narrators telling their version of it, but it clears itself up as the game goes on.

I've got so much other Final Fantasy to play that it'll be a while before I replay this one. The achievements are a bit grindy, but the real grind is just powering up enough to overcome Ruby and Emerald Weapon. Being able to defeat them trivializes the entire end of the game.

It was a groundbreaking experience at the time and a ton of fun even today.
