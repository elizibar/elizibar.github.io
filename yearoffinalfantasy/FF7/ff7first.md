# Final Fantasy VII: The First Soldier

**System**: Android / iOS  
**Release Date**: November 17, 2021  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy VII Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff7series/)  
**Date Played**:  

### Verdict

Free-to-play mobile battle royale (third-person shooter with RPG elements) set in the Compilation of Final Fantasy VII. Global release. Servers shut down on January 11, 2023 after a little over a year of service. Completely unplayable now. The related story content was later expanded in Ever Crisis.
