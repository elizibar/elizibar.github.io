# Final Fantasy Brick Breaker

**System**: DoCoMo i-mode (DoJa / i-appli)  
**Release Date**: Unknown (Japanese feature phone era; FF Appli)  
**Related Sub-Series**:  
**Date Played**: September 13, 2026  

### Verdict

Surprisingly fun.

It's Breakout with a pile of entertaining power-ups, and the big surprise was being able to move the paddle in four directions instead of only left and right. That alone made it feel fresher than a lot of straight Breakout clones.

There were only three stages in what we played. That may have been a demo build or a limited version, but more stages would have been welcome. Difficulty sat a little on the easy side.

I wouldn't really "recommend" it in a practical sense - you can't play it on modern phones anyway - but it was a good time while it lasted. No need to replay it. It is what it is, and the world is full of other Breakout games.

On the title screen it’s “FF Brick Breaker Part 1,” which lines up with only getting three stages - either a short release or the first part of a multi-part download.
