# Final Fantasy Resonance Demo

**System**: Steam (PC)  
**Release Date**: September 3, 2026  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy Resonance](/yearoffinalfantasy/ffrseries/)  
**Date Played**: September 7, 2026 through September 12, 2026

### Verdict

This was a blast. I really enjoyed the 2.5D presentation - it let them do some cool camera work without going fully 3D, and after so much FF7-based Final Fantasy it felt refreshing. I'd never played Octopath or anything else in this style, so the whole look and feel was new to me.

I wasn't sure what to expect going in, but the demo delivered. The Visions system looked suspicious on paper until I actually got to use it. Mixing and matching different types of characters and Visions drove both customization and character growth in a way that felt creative and fun. There's also a moment at the archaeological dig site that hit me harder than I expected - no spoilers, but it got some real feels out of me. Some of the writing dragged in places, but overall it was a great breath of fresh air after Dirge of Cerberus.

Difficulty was fine. The first couple of random battles felt a bit rough, and there are two optional encounters later that need some smart play, but nothing unfair. Menus were quick and responsive, some of the music was great, and making the tutorial the foundational backstory of the game worked really well - big stuff was happening and you got to feel powerful right away. I was surprised how much game they packed into a free demo. I got about seven hours out of it.

It almost feels like a "Greatest Hits of Final Fantasy" game with how many threads and characters it pulls in from other sources. Seems like it might follow a path similar to Final Fantasy V, which has me intrigued. I'd recommend the demo to anyone who's on the fence - try it and see for yourself. No achievements, and the optional fights were doable. I expect to pick up the full version later, maybe in a year or so after we've gotten through more of the other Final Fantasy games.
