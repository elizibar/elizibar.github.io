# Final Fantasy: Unlimited

*Final Fantasy: Unlimited* (often shortened to *FF:U*) is a 2001-2002 anime series produced by Gonzo with Square (later Square Enix) involvement. It is an original story rather than an adaptation of any existing game. Akitoshi Kawazu provided the base concept; Mahiro Maeda directed. The show mixes 2D animation and 3D CG and borrows familiar Final Fantasy flavor (summons, Chocobos, Moogles, a Cid, a Magun that functions a bit like a Gunblade, etc.) while telling its own tale.

The plot follows twelve-year-old twins Ai and Yu Hayakawa as they enter a parallel dimension called Wonderland searching for their missing parents. They travel with Lisa Pacifist (an operative of the C2 Organization) and later cross paths with the amnesiac Unlimited known as Kaze (the "Black Wind") and the sword-wielding Unlimited Makenshi. Opposing them are the Lords of Gaudium and their master, Earl Tyrant / Chaos, who seeks the fragments of a destructive being called Omega.

It was originally planned for 52 episodes. Low ratings and the box-office failure of *Final Fantasy: The Spirits Within* led to the run being cut in half. The series aired on TV Tokyo from October 2, 2001 to March 26, 2002 for a total of 25 episodes and ended on an incomplete note. ADV Films licensed it for North America and the UK and released it on DVD (individual volumes and later a complete collection). It has never received a wide official streaming release in the West.

### Continuation media

Because the anime stopped midway, the story continued in Japanese-only print, audio, and game formats:

- **Final Fantasy: Unlimited - Sou no Kizuna** (novel, 2002) - side story set during the TV series.
- **Final Fantasy: Unlimited Before** (drama CD) - backstory covering the destruction of Kaze and Makenshi's original worlds and their first clash with Chaos.
- **Final Fantasy: Unlimited After** (book with script + manga, 2002) - events after the anime; the twins and Lisa return to Earth and the C2 Organization is explored further. Introduces the new antagonist Soljashy.
- **Final Fantasy: Unlimited After Spiral** (web novel series) - further post-series adventures, including material on Makenshi's past.
- **Final Fantasy: Unlimited After 2** (radio drama) - Comodeen's final push against Gaudium and resolution of the Lisa/Soljashy conflict.
- **Final Fantasy: Unlimited with U** (2002, Japanese mobile) - turn-based RPG that retells the anime plot.
- **Final Fantasy: Unlimited on PC - Labyrinth: Black Dream Memories** (2003, Windows, Japan only) - visual novel / card-battle hybrid set in the same universe. An English fan translation has been in the works in recent years.

There was also a prologue DVD (Phase.0), art books, and soundtrack albums. Nobuo Uematsu contributed theme music; the bulk of the score was handled by Shirō Hamaguchi and Akifumi Tada. Opening theme is "Over the FANTASY."

### Reception

Contemporary Japanese rankings put it mid-pack. Western reviews were mixed to cool. Some praised the ambition, visual mix, and care taken with the characters; others found it too Saturday-morning, formulaic, or simply not living up to the weight the Final Fantasy name usually carries. The abrupt truncation did not help. It remains one of the more obscure corners of the franchise - remembered by people who caught the ADV DVDs in the mid-2000s, largely invisible to everyone else.
