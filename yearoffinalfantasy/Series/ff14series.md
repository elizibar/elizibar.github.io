# Final Fantasy XIV Series

*Final Fantasy XIV* is the fourteenth mainline entry in the series and Square Enix’s second major MMO after Final Fantasy XI. It has one of the most dramatic development histories in the franchise.

### Version 1.0 and the Shutdown

The original version of Final Fantasy XIV launched in September 2010 to extremely poor reviews. The game suffered from clumsy controls, sparse content, severe technical problems, and a general lack of direction. Player trust collapsed quickly.

In 2011 Square Enix put Naoki Yoshida (commonly known as Yoshi-P) in charge of the project. Rather than quietly shutting the game down, the team continued to support the existing version while secretly rebuilding it from the ground up. In November 2012 the original servers were shut down in a dramatic in-game event: the Calamity, in which the dragon Bahamut was unleashed and the world of Eorzea was devastated. Character data was preserved for the relaunch.

### A Realm Reborn

*Final Fantasy XIV: A Realm Reborn* launched on August 27, 2013. It was effectively a new game built on the ashes of the old one, with heavily revised systems, a much stronger story, and far better technical foundations. The relaunch was a critical and commercial success and is widely regarded as one of the most impressive turnarounds in modern game development.

Since then the game has received major expansions roughly every two years, each continuing the main story and adding new areas, jobs, and systems. The player base has grown substantially over time, and the game remains one of the most active and highly regarded MMOs.

### Yoshi-P and Communication

A major factor in the recovery was Yoshida’s approach to the community. He began writing regular “Letters from the Producer,” appeared in live letters and fan events, and spoke with unusual frankness about the game’s problems and the team’s plans. This open style of communication helped rebuild trust after the disaster of 1.0 and has remained a defining feature of the game’s relationship with its players.

### Expansions (Overview)

The major expansions so far include:

- *A Realm Reborn* (2.0, 2013) – the relaunch
- *Heavensward* (3.0, 2015)
- *Stormblood* (4.0, 2017)
- *Shadowbringers* (5.0, 2019)
- *Endwalker* (6.0, 2021)
- *Dawntrail* (7.0, 2024)

Each expansion has continued the overarching story while shifting tone, setting, and gameplay focus.

### Reception and Legacy

Final Fantasy XIV is now frequently cited as one of the best MMOs available and one of the strongest modern Final Fantasy experiences, particularly for its story and community. The journey from the failure of 1.0 to the success of A Realm Reborn and beyond is one of the most notable redemption stories in the industry.
