# Final Fantasy IX Series

*Final Fantasy IX* (2000) is the ninth mainline entry in the series. It was developed by Square for the PlayStation and is often described as a return to the series’ roots after the more modern styling of VII and VIII. The game follows Zidane Tribal and a colorful cast of characters (including Vivi, Garnet/Dagger, Steiner, Freya, Quina, Eiko, and Amarant) as they become involved in a conflict that spans multiple worlds and ultimately threatens existence itself. It is widely praised for its characters, fairy-tale tone, and soundtrack.

Like Final Fantasy VIII, IX does not have a large extended multimedia universe.

### Ports and Remasters

The original PlayStation version was later joined by a remaster released in 2016 for PC and mobile, then brought to PlayStation 4, Nintendo Switch, and Xbox One in subsequent years. The remaster includes higher-resolution character models, quality-of-life features, and various boosters. On PC it is also popular with community mods (most notably the Moguri Mod) that further improve visuals and presentation.

### Tetra Master

Tetra Master is the collectible card minigame in Final Fantasy IX (sometimes confused with Triple Triad from VIII). An expanded online version, *Tetra Master From Final Fantasy IX*, was released in 2002 and ran as a free-to-play multiplayer card game associated with *Final Fantasy XI* and the PlayOnline service. That online version shut down at the end of 2010.

### Other Notes

Characters from Final Fantasy IX have appeared in crossover titles such as the *Dissidia Final Fantasy* series. In recent years there have also been periodic rumors and reports about a possible remake and an animated series project, though neither has been fully confirmed as a released product at the time of writing.

Reception for the main game is generally very positive. Many players consider it one of the strongest entries in the series for its characters, emotional tone, and classic Final Fantasy feel. The remaster is widely regarded as a convenient way to play it on modern platforms, especially with community enhancements on PC.
