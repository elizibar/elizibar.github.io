# Final Fantasy VII Series

*Final Fantasy VII* (1997) is the seventh mainline entry in the series and one of the most influential RPGs ever made. Originally released on the PlayStation, it follows Cloud Strife and a group of rebels as they confront the Shinra Electric Power Company and the larger threat of Sephiroth and the Planet itself. The game introduced many players to cinematic storytelling in games, a large open world after the Midgar section, and a memorable cast of characters. It has been re-released and remastered many times across platforms.

Square Enix later expanded the world through the *Compilation of Final Fantasy VII*, a multimedia project that includes prequels, sequels, films, novels, and mobile games. More recently the story has been reimagined in the ongoing *Final Fantasy VII Remake* project.

### Prequels and Early Compilation Titles

**Before Crisis: Final Fantasy VII** (2004-2007, mobile, Japan only) focuses on the Turks in the years leading up to the original game. It was an episodic mobile title that never received an official Western release.

**Crisis Core: Final Fantasy VII** (2007, PSP; remastered as *Crisis Core -Final Fantasy VII- Reunion* in 2022) is a prequel centered on Zack Fair. It expands on the Nibelheim incident and Zack’s relationship with Cloud and Aerith. The Reunion version modernizes the graphics, combat, and voice acting to better match the Remake project.

### Advent Children and Related Media

**Final Fantasy VII: Advent Children** (2005, with the expanded *Advent Children Complete* in 2009) is a CGI film set two years after the original game. It follows Cloud as he deals with the lingering effects of Geostigma and a new threat connected to Sephiroth’s remnants.

**Last Order: Final Fantasy VII** (2005) is a short OVA depicting the Nibelheim incident. It was originally released alongside early versions of Advent Children and is closely tied to that project.

**On the Way to a Smile** is a collection of short stories by Kazushige Nojima that bridge the original game and Advent Children. One of these stories was adapted into the OVA *On the Way to a Smile - Episode: Denzel*, which was included with Advent Children Complete.

**The Kids Are Alright: A Turks Side Story** (novel) explores events involving the Turks and younger characters in the years around Advent Children.

### Dirge of Cerberus

**Dirge of Cerberus: Final Fantasy VII** (2006, PlayStation 2) is a third-person shooter that focuses on Vincent Valentine in the years after Advent Children. It deals with the remnants of the Deepground project and has a more action-oriented style than the rest of the series. A mobile spin-off, *Dirge of Cerberus Lost Episode*, also existed for a time.

### The Remake Project

**Final Fantasy VII Remake** (2020) reimagines the Midgar portion of the original game with modern action combat, expanded character moments, and new story developments. *Intergrade* (2021) is the PS5 version that added the Yuffie-focused *INTERmission* episode.

**Final Fantasy VII Rebirth** (2024) continues the story after the party leaves Midgar, expanding the world and further diverging from (and commenting on) the original narrative.

A third game in the Remake project is planned to complete the retelling.

Related novels such as *Traces of Two Pasts* expand on character backstories in the context of the Remake continuity.

### Mobile and Other Titles

**Final Fantasy VII Ever Crisis** (2023, mobile) is a gacha-style game that retells stories from across the Compilation and adds new scenarios, including material related to *The First Soldier*.

**Final Fantasy VII: The First Soldier** (2021-2023, mobile) was a battle-royale style game set in the early days of SOLDIER. Service has ended.

Other smaller releases include the mobile *Final Fantasy VII G-Bike* and various minor tie-in media.

### Appearances in Other Games

Characters from Final Fantasy VII have appeared in a number of other Square titles. Cloud, Tifa, and Sephiroth are playable or featured in the fighting game *Ehrgeiz: God Bless the Ring* (1998). Cloud and Sephiroth have also been recurring guests in the *Dissidia Final Fantasy* series, *Super Smash Bros.* (Cloud), and various crossover and rhythm games over the years.

### Notable Fan Works

The popularity of Final Fantasy VII has produced a large body of fan work. Among the more widely known projects are *Voices of the Lifestream*, a high-profile arranged and progressive-metal tribute album, and various demakes such as the fan-made NES-style version of the game. These works are unofficial but have maintained a visible presence in the community for years.

Reception across the Compilation varies widely. Crisis Core and Advent Children are generally the best-regarded of the earlier spin-offs. Dirge of Cerberus and many of the mobile titles have more mixed or niche reputations. The Remake project has been both praised for its production values and character work and debated for the ways it changes the original story.
