# Fabula Nova Crystallis

*Fabula Nova Crystallis Final Fantasy* is a collection of games that share a common mythology centered on crystals, gods, l'Cie, fal'Cie, and the tension between fate and free will. The project was announced in the mid-2000s as a multi-game universe, similar in ambition to the Compilation of Final Fantasy VII, though it ultimately took a more fragmented path.

The core myth involves the gods Pulse, Lindzei, and Etro, the creator god Bhunivelze, and the recurring idea of people marked as l'Cie and given a Focus they must complete or be punished for failing.

### The Lightning Trilogy

The most fully realized part of the project is the trilogy focused on Lightning:

- **Final Fantasy XIII** (2009) - The first release. Follows Lightning and her companions after they are marked as l'Cie and drawn into the conflict between Cocoon and Pulse.
- **Final Fantasy XIII-2** (2011) - Continues the story with Serah and Noel traveling through time to resolve paradoxes and confront Caius Ballad.
- **Lightning Returns: Final Fantasy XIII** (2013) - The conclusion, set centuries later, with Lightning tasked with saving souls before the end of the world.

These three games form a continuous narrative arc and are the clearest expression of the Fabula Nova Crystallis mythology in practice.

### Type-0 and the Agito Project

**Final Fantasy Type-0** (originally announced as *Final Fantasy Agito XIII*) is a real-time action RPG set in the world of Orience. It follows Class Zero, a group of students fighting in a war between nations that are themselves manipulated by larger forces. While it shares the crystal and l'Cie/fal'Cie terminology, it is largely self-contained and was eventually released as *Type-0* rather than carrying the XIII name.

A free-to-play mobile/online spin-off, *Final Fantasy Agito*, also existed for a time before shutting down. *Final Fantasy Type-0 HD* later brought the main game to more platforms.

### Versus XIII and Final Fantasy XV

**Final Fantasy Versus XIII** was the third major pillar of the original Fabula Nova Crystallis announcement. Over a long development period it was significantly reworked and eventually released as *Final Fantasy XV*. By the time it launched, most of the shared mythology had been removed or heavily altered, and it is generally treated as its own separate entry rather than a true Fabula Nova Crystallis title.

### Legacy

Fabula Nova Crystallis never became the tightly connected multi-game universe that was originally suggested. The Lightning trilogy stands as its main completed story, Type-0 remains a related but distinct experience, and Versus XIII evolved into something else entirely. The shared mythological language (l'Cie, fal'Cie, crystals, gods of creation and death) is the clearest thread tying the project together.

Among fans the Lightning trilogy in particular remains one of the more polarizing corners of modern Final Fantasy, praised by some for its combat, production values, and mythic ambition, and criticized by others for its storytelling and structure.
