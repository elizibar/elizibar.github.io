# Final Fantasy Resonance Series

*Final Fantasy Resonance* is the first HD-2D game in the Final Fantasy series. It takes the opening story arc of the mobile free-to-play title *Final Fantasy Brave Exvius* and rebuilds it from the ground up as a premium single-player console RPG.

*Final Fantasy Brave Exvius* launched in Japan on October 22, 2015 and worldwide on June 30, 2016. It was a gacha-driven mobile game with pixel art, turn-based battles, and a large cast of "Visions" drawn from across the Final Fantasy series. The global version shut down on October 30, 2024. The Japanese version followed on October 31, 2025. Once the servers went dark, the story content became unavailable to new players.

Resonance uses the first major story arc of Brave Exvius (often called the Lapis arc) as its foundation. Square Enix and co-developer Lancarse have been clear that this is not a straight port or simple remaster. The world, systems, and presentation were extensively rebuilt for consoles and PC. The result is a traditional turn-based RPG with modern HD-2D visuals, a fixed party instead of gacha pulls, and no stamina or live-service elements. Players can still call on Visions of classic Final Fantasy characters in battle, and side content such as Gilgamesh and optional super-bosses has been carried over or expanded.

A free demo covering the entire first chapter launched on September 3, 2026. Save data from the demo carries over to the full game. The complete version is scheduled for October 22, 2026 on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC (Steam and Microsoft Store).
