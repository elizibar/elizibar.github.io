# Final Fantasy XV Series

*Final Fantasy XV* (2016) is the fifteenth mainline entry in the series. It began life as *Final Fantasy Versus XIII* under the Fabula Nova Crystallis umbrella before being reworked into a standalone title. The game follows Noctis Lucis Caelum and his three companions (Gladiolus, Ignis, and Prompto) on a road trip that turns into a struggle for the fate of the kingdom of Lucis and the world. It emphasizes open-world driving and exploration, real-time action combat, and a strong focus on the friendship between the four leads.

The game launched with a somewhat incomplete story and received substantial post-launch support.

### Kingsglaive and Brotherhood

**Kingsglaive: Final Fantasy XV** (2016) is a CGI feature film that serves as a prequel to the game. It focuses on the fall of Insomnia, the Kingsglaive soldiers, and the political machinations surrounding the peace treaty with Niflheim. Several key events and character motivations in the main game are much clearer with the film as context.

**Brotherhood: Final Fantasy XV** is a short anime series (also 2016) that explores the backstories and relationships of Noctis and his friends in the years leading up to the game. It is lighter in tone and helps flesh out the party dynamics.

### Downloadable Content

Final Fantasy XV received a series of single-player story DLC episodes focused on individual characters:

- **Episode Gladiolus**
- **Episode Prompto**
- **Episode Ignis**
- **Episode Ardyn** (the final major story DLC, exploring the villain’s past)

These episodes expand on character backstories and fill in some of the gaps left by the main game. A larger planned multi-part DLC project (sometimes referred to in connection with *The Dawn of the Future*) was ultimately cancelled; an alternate-story novel was released instead.

### Ports and Later Versions

The game received a Windows Edition and later a Royal Edition that incorporated many of the updates, new content, and quality-of-life improvements. It is available on PlayStation 4, Xbox One, and PC.

### Reception

Reception for Final Fantasy XV has been mixed-to-positive. Many players praise the chemistry between the four main characters, the road-trip atmosphere, the combat, and the soundtrack. Common criticisms focus on the uneven pacing, the originally incomplete feeling of the main story, and some late-game design choices. The additional movies, anime, and DLC are generally considered helpful for understanding the full narrative, though the fragmented release approach is sometimes cited as a drawback.
