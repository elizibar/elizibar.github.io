# Final Fantasy IV Pixel Remaster

**System**: Steam (PC)  
**Release Date**: September 8, 2021  
**Related Sub-Series**: [Final Fantasy IV Series](/yearoffinalfantasy/ff4series/)  
**Date Played**: February 22, 2026 through February 28, 2026  

### Verdict

Loved it. The Pixel Remaster quality-of-life updates make an already fun experience even more fun.

Final Fantasy IV delivers a solid story, good music, good graphics, and good gameplay. It is hard to single out one standout element when so much of it lands. It was not the first SNES Final Fantasy I played (that was the one released as Final Fantasy III in North America), but coming back to it years later really drives home how big a leap it was from the NES games onto the new hardware.

I expected to have a blast. I had an even bigger blast than I expected.

This is an easy recommendation with no reservations. There are too many good decisions to list: Cecil and Kain both working through their own redemption arcs, the exploration of love and consequences, Tellah’s revenge, and more. The story and characters are excellent.

The one clear annoyance is the game’s obsession with extremely rare encounters for rare loot. Things like the Pink Flans or the Mindflayer summon simply do not respect your time.

Relative to the first six games, this is probably the second-best Final Fantasy, and that is before the Pixel Remaster enhancements. The soundtrack slaps; the Lunar theme in particular carries that otherworldly feel perfectly. Difficulty sits a bit on the easy side even without intentional overleveling, but a few fights can still surprise a strong party.

I have a lot of other things I should play, but I might come back to this one. I should check whether Free Enterprise-style randomizer tools work with the Pixel Remaster; that could be neat. The optional rare drops do set a bad tone for 100% completion, though. The grind required for full completion is ludicrous.
